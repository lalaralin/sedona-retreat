The Adventures

Hikes

Sedona's red rock trails are calling. We'll tackle two iconic hikes during the weekend — bring your sense of adventure.

Cathedral Rock

Distance
1.2 mi
Elev. Gain
740 ft
Difficulty
Hard

One of Sedona's most iconic hikes — a steep scramble up red rock slickrock to jaw-dropping 360° views of the valley. Short but intense, and absolutely worth it.

Devil's Bridge

Distance
4.2 mi
Elev. Gain
560 ft
Difficulty
Moderate

Sedona's largest natural sandstone arch. The trail winds through juniper forest before revealing the dramatic bridge — the ultimate photo op.

Details & Travel

Getting There

Fly into Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX) — it's the closest major airport, about a 2-hour scenic drive north through Arizona's red rock country.

Once you book your flight, let Lara know! She'll coordinate carpools and rental cars so you don't have to worry about a thing.

What to Know

Bring your favorite yoga gear, swimsuit, layers for cool desert evenings, and a good book.

Meals will be a mix of group cooking and catered experiences. We'll sort out the details together.

Questions? Reach out anytime — this is meant to be effortless for you.
