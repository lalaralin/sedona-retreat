Good to Know

Sedona Tips

🎟
Red Rock Pass
Most trailheads need a Red Rock Pass ($5/day) or an America the Beautiful pass on the dash. Kiosks at the lots take cards.
🚌
Sedona Shuttle
Thu–Sun, some popular trailheads (Cathedral Rock included) are shuttle-only during the day. Free — check sedonashuttle.com before you drive.
💧
Water, Always
It's high desert at 4,300 ft — dry air and strong sun. Carry more water than you think, even for the short stuff.
🚗
Grab a Car Buddy
Cars are shared, so coordinate in the group chat before heading out — and let Lara know so nobody's stranded for the hike.
