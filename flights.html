Good to Know

Getting to Sedona

🚗

PHX → Sedona

The drive from Phoenix Sky Harbor to Sedona is about 2 hours via I-17 N. We'll coordinate carpooling or rental cars closer to the date.

⏰

Arrive by Noon

Try to land by late morning so we can all arrive at the retreat house by early afternoon for intention setting and our first sunset together.

💡

Book Early

Prices shown are approximate as of early July 2026. September is still warm season in AZ — flights tend to go up closer to the date!
